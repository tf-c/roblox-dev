# Throw-to-collect game

A throw-to-collect loop: the player throws a Toy down the Lane, a Creature arrives where it lands and goes to the player's Plot. The theme (today: catboys and a yarn ball) is swappable; the loop is not.

## Language

### The loop

**Toy**:
The object the player throws down the Lane. It lands on the ground, never on a Creature. Today it is a yarn ball.
_Avoid_: Seed, projectile (in player-facing text), food

**Lane**:
The straight stretch of ground the player throws along, split into Zones by distance.
_Avoid_: Field, track

**Zone**:
A distance band of the Lane that decides which Creatures can arrive. Today, nearest to farthest: Backyard, Park, Meadow.
_Avoid_: Area, biome

**Arrival**:
A Creature coming from the edge of a Zone to where the Toy landed.
_Avoid_: Sprout, spawn, hatch, reveal

**Creature**:
What arrives at a landed Toy and goes to the player's Plot to earn cash. Catboy is the current theme's name for it.
_Avoid_: Critter, pet, unit

**Catboy**:
The current theme's Creature: an original, friendly, fully clothed cat-eared character.
_Avoid_: Using it for the loop concept itself (say Creature)

**Plot**:
The player's own pedestals where collected Creatures sit and earn cash per second.
_Avoid_: Base, farm, garden
